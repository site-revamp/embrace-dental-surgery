# emBRACE Dental Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

emBRACE Dental Surgery is a Singapore-based specialist orthodontic clinic located at International Building, Orchard Road. Led by Dr Tan Kok Liang, the practice provides orthodontic treatments including non-ligating braces, traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, lingual braces, and Invisalign clear aligners.

The redesigned homepage focuses on establishing a stronger specialist orthodontic identity, simplifying treatment discovery, and highlighting clinical expertise through a modern, welcoming, and consultation-focused digital experience.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Specialist Orthodontic Positioning

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage did not communicate the clinic's specialist orthodontic positioning and consultation pathways clearly enough at first glance.

**Redesign Improvement:** Strengthened the hero section with clearer orthodontic-focused messaging, supporting information, and prominent consultation actions. The updated introduction highlights the clinic's specialist expertise and helps visitors understand its primary services and next steps more immediately.

### 2. Improved Braces and Aligner Treatment Discovery

**Previous Issue:** The clinic's range of orthodontic treatments needed a clearer, more accessible presentation to help visitors understand and explore their available options.

**Redesign Improvement:** Organised the main braces and clear-aligner options into a structured 2 × 2 treatment grid. This creates a more scannable introduction to the clinic's orthodontic services, helping patients identify relevant treatment pathways without unnecessary navigation.

### 3. Stronger Clinical Expertise and Trust Communication

**Previous Issue:** Important trust-building information, including Dr Tan's specialist experience, treatment approach, and patient guidance, required greater prominence within the homepage experience.

**Redesign Improvement:** Integrated Dr Tan's professional profile, the clinic's patient-care promises, a clearer treatment journey, and frequently asked questions into a more cohesive homepage structure. These elements provide reassurance and relevant information closer to the consultation actions, helping visitors make more informed decisions.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Established a stronger progression from specialist-focused messaging to treatment options, clinical expertise, and consultation opportunities.
- **Improved Treatment Presentation:** Organised the main orthodontic treatments into a structured 2 × 2 grid for easier browsing and comparison.
- **Stronger Specialist Identity:** Reinforced the clinic's orthodontic focus while preserving the existing emBRACE brand direction.
- **Enhanced Trust Communication:** Increased the prominence of Dr Tan's professional experience, clinic promises, and patient guidance.
- **Clearer Consultation Pathways:** Positioned consultation actions more prominently throughout the patient journey.
- **Improved Information Accessibility:** Incorporated treatment guidance and FAQs to help visitors understand their options before contacting the clinic.
- **Consistent Visual Presentation:** Emphasised clean layouts, balanced spacing, readable typography, and a cohesive, clinically professional appearance.

## Summary

The redesigned emBRACE Dental Surgery homepage addresses three primary communication and usability challenges: unclear specialist positioning, limited treatment discoverability, and insufficient prominence of clinical expertise and patient reassurance.

The improvements establish a clearer, more informative, and consultation-focused patient experience while reinforcing the clinic's commitment to specialist orthodontic care, treatment innovation, patient comfort, and personalised guidance.
