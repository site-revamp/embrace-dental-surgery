Helpful information

Questions before you begin?

Here are a few common questions about starting orthodontic treatment.

What happens during the first orthodontic consultation?
Your first visit includes an examination of your teeth, bite and jaw alignment. X-rays or digital scans may be taken where needed to help determine a suitable treatment plan.
How long does orthodontic treatment usually take?
Treatment duration depends on the complexity of your case and the movements required. The clinic's published guidance gives an average range of 12 to 24 months, but your orthodontist can advise after assessing your needs.
Can adults get braces or clear aligners?
Yes. Orthodontic treatment is not limited to teenagers. Adults can discuss braces and aligner options with the orthodontist based on their teeth, bite and treatment goals.
How often will I need to visit the clinic?
Appointment frequency varies by treatment type and individual progress. The clinic's general guidance describes visits every four to eight weeks for many orthodontic treatments. Your orthodontist will advise on your schedule.
How much does braces treatment cost?
Pricing depends on the type of braces and your treatment needs. emBRACE publishes package information and states that its package prices are nett prices. Contact the clinic for current fees and inclusions before making a decision.
